# drone-capture-hardware

## Summary

Scope: drone capture protocol, drone class, and processing load on an RTX 3080 Ti laptop (16 GB) for a ~10x10 m forest plot (larger tropical plots later). Note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through, so a few late items (Autel specs, whether BLK2FLY is still sold, EUR prices, the US FCC status of DJI) were not verified. They are flagged as low confidence.

1) What the drone can contribute to a 10x10 m plot. At 30-40 m above the canopy, one 24 mm-equivalent 4:3 frame covers about 1.44D x 1.08D (D = camera-to-canopy distance), roughly 45-58 m x 33-43 m. So the whole plot fits in one image. Grid coverage is easy. What matters is angular diversity, and most of the plot floor is hidden by canopy. Splat-oriented guidance agrees that nadir-only flights give poor splats. You need oblique crosshatch passes plus multi-altitude, variable-radius orbits (WebODM docs: "a nadir-only flight usually yields very poor results"; Pix4D; MipMap; ESRI). Overlap targets for splats are at least 80/70, with 85/80 for best quality. Forest photogrammetry needs more. Pix4D says at least 85% front and 70-85% side for dense vegetation and recommends flying higher. Frey et al. 2018 got the best forest reconstruction completeness with more than 95% overlap and GSD coarser than 5 cm. Dandois et al. 2015 tested 20-80 m above canopy: canopy-height error doubled when forward overlap dropped, and accuracy needed more than 80% overlap. Recommended protocol for a small plot (about 300-550 stills, about 15-30 min of flying, 1-2 batteries):
- Nadir crosshatch at canopy+40-60 m over a 60-100 m AOI, 85-90/75-80 overlap.
- Oblique crosshatch at -45°.
- 2-3 orbits around the plot centre at a 5° step (72 photos per ring), for example R≈20 m @ canopy+15 m with gimbal -50°, R≈35 m @ +30 m with -40°, and R≈50 m @ +45 m with -30°.
- A bridge sequence: a vertical "elevator" ascent and descent in the nearest gap or at the forest edge, with photos every 1-2 m from about 2 m up to canopy+10 m, plus slow near-horizontal passes (-10 to -20°) along the edge facing in. This copies heliguy's M4E + XGRIDS fusion workflow, which used images every 1 m during the ascent plus takeoff/landing shots for ground-to-aerial registration.

Under-canopy flight is not advisable. DJI vision sensing does not reliably detect branches thinner than about 2-3 cm, and the pilot has to keep visual line of sight from a clearing.

2) Stills versus video, and camera settings. Prefer stills in 12 MP mode on Mini and Air models:
- faster readout (Air 3S under 16 ms at 12 MP versus 20-30 ms at 50 MP; Mini 3 Pro 26 ms versus 60 ms)
- the 2 s minimum interval applies only at 12 MP; at 48/50 MP the minimum is 5 s
- smaller files.

Use DJI Fly waypoints with hover-and-shoot (up to 200 waypoints, hover 0-30 s) or a slow interval-timer flight (1-3 m/s), which makes rolling shutter irrelevant. The M4E's mechanical shutter (up to 1/2000 s) and 0.5 s interval matter only for fast, large-area grids, for example the later tropical plots.

Video (4K at 130-150 Mbps, about 1 GB/min, 8.3 MP frames, H.265 compression, no per-frame EXIF GPS, only SRT telemetry) is acceptable for a quick first test with frames extracted at 1-2 fps, but stills are better.

Settings:
- Manual exposure, ISO 100-400, shutter at least 1/1000 s when moving (at least 1/500 s when hovering).
- White balance fixed in Kelvin; focus locked; HDR/SmartPhoto modes off; 4:3 aspect, uncropped.
- JPEG is enough. JPEG+DNG is useful if you want to match colour with the Insta360.
- Distortion correction OFF (DewarpFlag=0), so the SfM lens model sees unaltered optics. Never mix corrected and uncorrected images.

Fly within the same overcast window as the ground walk, directly before or after it (within 30-60 min), with wind at the canopy below about 3-4 m/s. Dandois found clear sky gives slightly deeper canopy penetration (18% vs 16%), but diffuse light is better for matching appearance with under-canopy 360 video (the under-canopy NeRF paper, WACV-W 2026). Sequence the drone and ground captures so the ground operator does not appear in the aerial frames.

3) Drone class. What matters, in order:
(1) Automated missions: MSDK support, or DJI Fly waypoints/KMZ.
(2) A sensor of about 1 inch with fast readout, or a mechanical shutter.
(3) C0/C1 class for easy paperwork.
(4) RAW and manual exposure.
(5) RTK is optional. The BLK2GO has no GNSS, so RTK only georeferences the aerial layer. GCPs/targets in canopy gaps or the LiDAR geometry still tie it to the LiDAR frame.

Candidates:
- Budget: DJI Mini 4 Pro (C0, under 249 g). It is the only current Mini on MSDK v5, so Dronelink (full virtual-stick grids and orbits) and Litchi Pilot work. Alternatively the DJI Mini 5 Pro (C0 with the standard 249.9 g battery, C1 with the Plus battery; 1-inch 50 MP; forward LiDAR). It has no MSDK, so you use DJI Fly waypoints plus KMZ through Pixpro (about €5/month) or Litchi Hub.
- Mid: DJI Air 3S (C1, 724 g, 1-inch 50 MP plus 70 mm tele, 45 min). Dronelink supports it in onboard-waypoint mode only.
- Pro and tropical: DJI Matrice 4E (C2, 1219 g, 4/3 20 MP mechanical shutter, 0.5 s interval, integrated RTK, Smart 3D/oblique, MSDK, 49 min, about $5,000-5,400).
- Overkill for this: Mavic 4 Pro (C2, 1063 g, 100 MP). The Mavic 3E is being phased out.
- Non-DJI: Autel EVO II Pro V3 / RTK V3 (C2 by firmware, about $3k with RTK). Parrot ANAFI Ai is ageing and Pix4Dcapture has been terminated. Leica BLK2FLY is no longer listed on Leica's autonomous-capture page; check internally at Hexagon.

Regulations: Swedish operator registration and A1/A3 online training apply. A3 rules require 150 m from residential areas and a 120 m ceiling. Publishing aerial images or 3D models of Swedish land on the web needs a Lantmäteriet spridningstillstånd (dissemination permit). It is free, and there is a forest exemption when the landowner commissioned or took the imagery. Derived 3D models of approved material need no separate review.

4) Data and compute.
- File sizes (estimates): about 4-8 MB per JPEG at 12 MP, 7-12 MB at 20 MP, 15-25 MB at 48-50 MP. DNG is about 24 MB (12 MP), 40 MB (20 MP) and 70-100 MB (48-50 MP). So a plot is about 2-4 GB as JPEG, 12-50 GB with DNG, and about 1 GB per minute of 4K video.
- SfM: COLMAP downsizes to 3200 px for SIFT by default.
- Training: INRIA 3DGS auto-rescales images wider than 1.6K. Train aerial images at 1600-2000 px.
- VRAM: INRIA keeps every image on the GPU as float32 by default (about 23 MB per 1600x1200 image, so 300 aerial images ≈ 6.9 GB). Use --data_device cpu, or gsplat, which loads from disk or host RAM. On 16 GB, Gaussian count dominates: gsplat measured 5.6 GB for 3.24M Gaussians (default) and 2.0/5.0 GB for 1M/3M with MCMC; OpenSplat states about 2 GB per million. A ground plus aerial merged model should be capped at about 4-6M Gaussians (MCMC cap). Under a fixed cap, aerial context takes budget away from ground detail, and the much larger scene extent changes densification scaling. This is a compute argument for option (c): separate models, each with its own budget.
- Training time: roughly 30-60 min per 30k iterations on the laptop GPU (inferred from A100 and RTX 3060/4070 figures).
- ODM/ODX: OpenDroneMap is AGPL-3.0, runs natively on Windows or in Docker, and uses the GPU only for SIFT (about 2x faster). GPU on Windows effectively needs the Docker/WSL2 image. RAM scales with image count: about 16 GB for 250 images and 32 GB for 500. Since April 2026, WebODM (AGPL-3.0, free installer v3.3.0) has split off to its own ODX engine and exports splat training data for OpenSplat (AGPL-3.0). Use it as an external process for aerial ortho, DSM, CHM and splat layers.

Overall: keep the first test as LiDAR + Insta360. Add the drone later as option (c), a separate aerial layer, and capture the bridge images so a merge can be tried later if wanted.

## Recommendations

- **Keep Phase 1 as LiDAR + Insta360 only. Add the drone as a later, optional option (c): a separate aerial model (splat plus optional ortho, DSM and canopy-height model) registered into the LiDAR frame and shown as its own viewer layer.** — Over a 10x10 m plot the drone mainly sees canopy tops and surroundings. Merging into the ground splat (option b) needs aerial-to-ground SfM matching, which mostly fails off the shelf (AerialMegaDepth: about 5% registration success). It also splits a 16 GB VRAM Gaussian budget and stretches scene extent about 10x. Option (c) gives each model its own budget, can be processed with a separate pipeline (COLMAP/GLOMAP+gsplat or ODX/WebODM), and suits a modular source design. _Alternatives:_ (b) merged single splat: only if the bridge images register reliably. Treat it as an experiment after (c) works. (a) alone is enough for the forest-floor product.
- **Aerial capture protocol for the first drone test (about 300-550 stills, 15-30 min, 1-2 batteries): nadir crosshatch at canopy+40-60 m with 85-90/75-80 overlap; oblique crosshatch at -45°; 2-3 orbits at 5° steps (R≈20/35/50 m at canopy+15/30/45 m, gimbal -50/-40/-30°); and a bridge sequence (vertical ascent in a gap or at the forest edge with photos every 1-2 m from 2 m to canopy+10 m, plus near-horizontal edge passes).** — Combines the splat guidance (orbits and obliques; 80/70 minimum), the forest guidance (85%+ overlap; Frey >95%), and the proven fusion practice of vertical-ascent and takeoff/landing bridge images (heliguy M4E workflow). Orbits give the angular diversity splats need. The bridge images are the only views that overlap the ground capture, and they keep option (b) possible later. _Alternatives:_ Reduced set for a quick feasibility test: 3 orbits, one oblique pass and the bridge (about 250-300 images in one battery). Video: 4K at 1-2 m/s on orbits with 1-2 fps extraction, acceptable for a test only.
- **Camera settings: stills in 12 MP mode (not 48/50 MP), 4:3 uncropped, manual exposure, ISO 100-400, shutter at least 1/1000 s while moving (at least 1/500 s when hovering), fixed Kelvin white balance, focus locked, distortion correction OFF, HDR/SmartPhoto off, JPEG (+DNG optional). Use hover-and-shoot waypoints or flight at 1-3 m/s.** — 12 MP mode has 2-4x faster readout and a 2 s minimum interval (48/50 MP is limited to 5 s). Hover-and-shoot removes rolling-shutter and blur effects, which COLMAP does not model. Raw lens geometry keeps SfM intrinsics consistent. Fixed exposure and white balance avoid appearance flicker that splats turn into floaters or colour seams. _Alternatives:_ Matrice 4E: use the mechanical shutter at 1/1000-1/2000 s with a 0.5 s interval and fly at normal mapping speed.
- **Timing: fly the drone directly before or after the ground walk, within the same 30-60 min of uniform overcast, with canopy wind below about 3-4 m/s, and keep the ground operator out of the aerial frames.** — Diffuse light suits both under-canopy 360 video and appearance matching between sources. Moving foliage breaks SfM and causes splat ghosting. Dandois found clear sky gives slightly deeper SfM canopy penetration (18% vs 16%), but appearance consistency matters more for this viewer. _Alternatives:_ If it is sunny, fly near solar noon and keep the gap under about 30 min. Expect shadow mismatch and use per-image appearance embeddings in training.
- **Drone choice. Budget: DJI Mini 4 Pro (C0; MSDK, so Dronelink/Litchi Pilot can automate grids and orbits and you could write your own Android app) or DJI Mini 5 Pro (C0 with standard battery; better 1-inch sensor; DJI Fly waypoints + Pixpro/Litchi Hub KMZ). Mid: DJI Air 3S (C1). Pro or tropical scale: DJI Matrice 4E (C2, mechanical shutter, 0.5 s interval, integrated RTK, Smart 3D, MSDK; about $5-5.4k).** — For a 10x10 m plot, a sub-250 g drone with waypoint hover-and-shoot is enough and needs the least paperwork. A mechanical shutter and RTK pay off only for large, fast grids and georeferencing, as on later tropical plots. Without GNSS on the BLK2GO, RTK does not by itself tie the aerial model to the LiDAR frame. _Alternatives:_ Autel EVO II Pro (RTK) V3 (C2) if company policy excludes DJI. Mavic 4 Pro (C2, 100 MP) is overkill. Check internally whether Hexagon still offers BLK2FLY or any aerial capture hardware.
- **Place 4-6 high-contrast targets in canopy gaps or at the forest edge that are visible both from the air and to the BLK2GO and Insta360, and take drone takeoff/landing shots near them.** — A handheld LiDAR scanning from below and a drone viewing from above share little surface. Targets in gaps, plus stems at the plot edge seen in the low obliques, are the most reliable ties for registering the aerial model to the LiDAR frame. Drone GPS or RTK only gives a georeferenced frame and approximate scale. _Alternatives:_ Register the aerial model's DSM to LiDAR stems and gap edges with ICP and use GPS priors as an initial guess. This is less reliable under closed canopy.
- **Compute settings: never keep images on the GPU (gsplat, or INRIA with --data_device cpu); train aerial images at 1600-2000 px; let COLMAP downsize to 3200 px for SIFT; cap Gaussians with MCMC (about 1-3M for the aerial layer, and 4-6M total if merged); use COLMAP's spatial matcher with EXIF GPS for the drone set.** — 300 aerial images at 1600x1200 stored as float32 on the GPU take about 6.9 GB. Gaussian memory is about 1.7-2 GB per million. A 16 GB laptop GPU with Windows overhead realistically allows about 4-6M Gaussians. _Alternatives:_ ODX/WebODM (AGPL, run as an external process) for the aerial ortho, DSM and point cloud and for OpenSfM poses fed to OpenSplat or gsplat.
- **Before publishing any aerial layer of a Swedish site, get a Lantmäteriet spridningstillstånd (free), or confirm the forest/landowner exemption. Register as operator with Transportstyrelsen and do A1/A3 training, and fly in A3 conditions. Plan tropical permits per country.** — Aerial images and derived 3D models of Swedish land are covered by Lag 2016:319. A web-delivered splat counts as dissemination. _Alternatives:_ Keep the aerial layer private or internal until the permit is granted.

## risks

- Aerial and ground images may not register in one SfM run, because of the large viewpoint, scale and appearance gap (AerialMegaDepth shows about 5% registration success for off-the-shelf methods). Option (b) may fail outright, and even option (c) needs targets or LiDAR-geometry ties.
- Under a closed canopy the drone sees almost none of the 10x10 m forest floor, so it adds little to the main product while adding cost, permits and processing.
- Wind-driven foliage motion between frames, and between the aerial and ground captures, causes SfM failures and splat ghosting or floaters at the canopy.
- Changing sun and shadows between the drone flight and the ground walk cause visible seams. Uniform overcast or a back-to-back schedule is required.
- Consumer DJI rolling shutter (16-60 ms readout) plus interval shooting while moving distorts geometry. Mitigate with 12 MP mode, slow flight or hover-and-shoot.
- DJI Fly limits: 200 waypoints per mission, no grid tool, no KML import on several models. Mini 5 Pro, Air 3S and Mavic 4 Pro lack MSDK, so they depend on KMZ-replacement workarounds (Pixpro, Litchi Hub) that firmware updates can break.
- VRAM: a merged model can exceed 16 GB because of the larger extent and more Gaussians. INRIA's default of keeping images on the GPU alone can use 7+ GB with 300 aerial images.
- Flight safety: obstacle sensing does not see thin branches, keeping visual line of sight from inside a forest is hard, GNSS is degraded near the canopy, and tropical emergent trees (60 m+) require a high return-to-home altitude.
- Legal: a Swedish spridningstillstånd is needed before publishing aerial data on the web; nature reserves may have local drone bans (not verified); tropical countries need separate permits and import paperwork.
- Corporate or vendor risk: company policy or future regulation may restrict DJI. US FCC actions against foreign drones in 2025-26 come from background knowledge and were not verified. Non-DJI options are weaker or costlier.
- AGPL-3.0 (ODM, ODX, WebODM, OpenSplat): fine as separately invoked tools, but modifying them or embedding them in a distributed or networked product triggers source-sharing obligations.
- Several numbers were not verified because the shared web-search budget ran out: per-image file sizes, training times on the 3080 Ti laptop, Autel/Skydio details, BLK2FLY status, EUR prices.

## FACT-CHECK: recommendation assessment

The core recommendations hold up and are strengthened. Phase 1 stays LiDAR + Insta360. Drone comes later as option (c), a separately trained aerial model registered to the LiDAR frame by targets or GCPs, with bridge images captured so (b) can be tried later. Horizon-GS, which the report missed, reports that joint aerial+street training 'consistently underperformed separate training'. AerialMegaDepth confirms that off-the-shelf DUSt3R registers fewer than 5% of aerial-ground pairs within 5°. The protocol (obliques plus multi-radius orbits, 85-90/75-80 overlap for forest, 12 MP stills, hover-and-shoot or slow flight, distortion correction off, same overcast window) is consistent with the primary sources checked. Specs, class labels, intervals, MSDK and third-party app support, gsplat and OpenSplat memory figures, the INRIA data_device default, and ODM/ODX/WebODM licences are verified.

Changes I would make:
(1) Drop the claim that the bridge sequence 'copies' a proven workflow. heliguy's fusion depended on an RTK fix on both the K1 and the M4E at an open building site, and the BLK2GO has no GNSS. Under canopy, 4-6 targets in gaps or at the edge, visible to drone, BLK2GO and Insta360, are the primary tie. Bridge images are an experiment.
(2) Mini 4 Pro: say explicitly to buy the RC-N2 bundle, because MSDK does not work with the RC 2. In the EU it is 34 min only, since the Plus battery is not sold there. Mini 3 and Mini 3 Pro are also MSDK models, so 'only' should go.
(3) Matrice 4E: RTK is integrated and non-removable, and it is C2 (DJI FAQ). Remove the open question.
(4) Pixpro currently offers only single grids and orbits. Oblique multi-grids may not exist yet, so the budget-drone oblique crosshatch may need manual DJI Fly waypoints or Dronelink on the Mini 4 Pro.
(5) Fix the Swedish compliance section. Fees are 190 SEK plus 250 SEK per year. Flying a drone of 250 g or more without a drönarkort has been criminal since 1 Jan 2026. Work flights need liability insurance under EU 785/2004. Check nature-reserve and national-park take-off/landing bans with Länsstyrelsen and check LFV Drönarkartan.
(6) Fix the spridningstillstånd guidance. Allow about 65 working days and apply as soon as a site is chosen. 'Internal' sharing by email, corporate cloud or a hosted viewer already counts as dissemination, so the aerial layer must stay on the local laptop until approval or a confirmed landowner exemption. Submit images, not 3D models, which Lantmäteriet cannot retouch. This constrains the web-delivery and LuciadRIA plan for drone layers.
(7) Re-cite the WACV-W 2026 paper as drone-from-above understory evidence, not as evidence about 360 video.
(8) Use gsplat (Apache-2.0) as the default trainer. INRIA 3DGS is non-commercial only (LICENSE Section 5), which matters for a Hexagon project.
(9) Optionally add a 360-drone experiment (DJI Avata 360, Antigravity A1) to the later phase for edge and gap bridge passes that share the Insta360 camera model.

Still unverified: per-image file sizes, the Dandois 18%/16% figures, the M4E price, Autel's 2026 status, and the FCC status. The shared WebSearch budget was exhausted, so verification used direct fetches of primary pages only.
